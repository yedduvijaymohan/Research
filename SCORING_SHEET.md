# Idea Feasibility Scoring Sheet — Quick Reference

## How to use

For any business idea, score 1-10 on each dimension. Sum and average. Below 6 = reject. 6-7 = consider with caveats. 8+ = strong fit.

---

| Dimension | 1 (Bad) | 10 (Great) | Your Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical buildability** | Needs novel ML, hardware, 6+ months | Pure software, known patterns, 6-8 weeks | ___ |
| **Data feasibility** | Needs customer data to start | Public/scrapeable data available | ___ |

## Decision tree

- **Average ≥ 8**: Build it. Spend the next week on customer validation.
- **Average 6-7.9**: Promising but has gaps. Identify the lowest 2 scores. Can you redesign the idea to lift those?
- **Average < 6**: Reject. Don't fall in love with the topic, find another angle.

## Mandatory red flag check

If ANY of these are true, stop regardless of score:

- [ ] Requires SOC 2/HIPAA/PCI/CMMC for MVP
- [ ] Hardware inventory and shipping required
- [ ] Enterprise procurement committee is the buyer
- [ ] Trade shows required to be credible ($10-30K)
- [ ] Licensed professional needed full-time for MVP
- [ ] Network effects required to be useful
- [ ] Funded competitors with $5M+ raised + 3+ year head start

If you checked any box: reject the idea or find a workaround before proceeding.

## Verification before commitment (do these before writing code)

- [ ] Searched Reddit for customer complaints in this space
- [ ] Found and priced 3+ competitors
- [ ] Confirmed pricing power by finding what customers actually pay
- [ ] Identified specific places to find buyers (URLs, communities, events)
- [ ] Talked to 5 potential customers (not just read about them)
- [ ] Built a list of 50 prospect names with contact methods

If you can't complete this list, don't build yet.
